// Building

jo is my current focus: a personal AI assistant that understood context and took action before Instinct, Town, or OpenClaw showed up. Now everyone's racing to build one (Muse, Meta AI, the whole field), and we're still at it.

I've spent years building products people use. Now I'm building the assistant I wish I had: proactive and genuinely helpful.

Also building AI agents, and figuring out how humans and AI should work together.

// Journey

I'm a two-time YC founder with 15+ years in growth marketing. It started at Kabam. I joined as an intern in 2008, back when the company was still called Watercooler, and stayed through its pivot into social gaming.

My first startup was Second Wave Games (2011). It sold to World Golf Tour (later acquired by Topgolf), where I stayed on as Head of Mobile.

Then product marketing at MessageMe (2014), the mobile messaging app Yahoo acquired. I learned more about startups from co-founder Arjun Sethi than from anyone before him, and those lessons went into everything I built after.

At Yahoo I was a Product Manager for Growth & Emerging Products (2014–2016). I grew Yahoo Mail into a top-4 app overall through growth experimentation. That's where the growth playbook came from: test everything, keep what moves the needle, never ship without an experiment behind it.

Then I co-founded Farmstead (YC S16), an AI-powered "ghost grocer" that delivered groceries in under 60 minutes from small-format micro-warehouse hubs.

We used machine learning for demand forecasting and kept food waste under 5% (supermarkets average around 35%). It started as a Nextdoor post in Mountain View offering weekly milk, eggs, yogurt, and bread. Two hundred replies in two days. Then COVID hit, orders went 10x, and we scaled to a $10M+ run rate across five markets before winding down in 2023.
